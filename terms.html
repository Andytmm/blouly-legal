4. AI-generated cards, and their limits

Every card in Blouly comes from an AI model, in one of two ways. The cards bundled with the app were written ahead of release and read through by us before they shipped. Every other card is written on demand, by a model responding to the topic you typed, and nobody reviews it before you see it. The warnings below apply to both.

Cards can be wrong. AI models produce confident, well-written text that is sometimes inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or simply invented. The app presents cards as facts because that is the reading experience — but you should treat them as a starting point for your curiosity, not as a source of truth. Check anything that matters before relying on it.

Cards are not professional advice. Nothing in Blouly is medical, legal, financial, tax, safety or other professional advice, and no card should be acted on as if it were. If a topic matters to your health, your money or your legal position, talk to a qualified professional.

We do not warrant that any card is accurate, complete or fit for a particular purpose, and to the extent permitted by law we are not liable for any decision you make on the basis of one. Because the model generates each card fresh, two people asking about the same topic will get different cards, and we cannot reproduce a specific card on request.

Cards may also, on occasion, cover a topic in a way you find dull, odd or wide of the mark. That is the nature of the thing rather than a fault, though we would like to hear about it.

6. Usage limits

Writing a new card costs us money every time, so generation is a paid feature and it carries a limit. Generating new cards needs an active subscription — see section 7. As things stand a subscriber can generate up to 50 cards a day, counted against midnight in your own time zone rather than UTC.

Reading is never limited. The cards bundled with the app, and every card that has already been written for you, stay readable as often as you like — subscription or not.

Without a subscription you can still name topics of your own. We keep them on your device so they are waiting for you, but no cards are generated for them until you subscribe.

We may change these limits, and we may apply reasonable additional limits to protect the service from abuse or runaway cost. If a change would meaningfully reduce what a paying subscriber gets, we will tell you first.

Some features need a working internet connection. In particular, saving a card while offline is kept on your device but is not sent to our servers until you reconnect, so it may not appear on another device until then.
