Blouly is an AI microlearning app for iPhone. You name the things you are curious about and it writes you short cards to swipe through. These are the documents that govern it.

  • Privacy Policy What we collect, what stays on your phone, what gets sent to an AI provider to write your cards, and how to delete all of it.
  • Terms of Use Your account, the limits of AI-generated cards, acceptable use, subscriptions, and who is responsible for what.
  • Subprocessors The four companies that process data on our behalf, exactly what each one receives, and where it is stored.

In brief

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking. The app collects no advertising identifier, and contains exactly one third-party code library — RevenueCat's, which is there to make subscriptions work.
  • Your topic names are sent to Anthropic to generate cards — nothing that identifies you goes with them.
  • Your blocked-app list and how often you open those apps never leave your phone.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, without emailing anyone.
